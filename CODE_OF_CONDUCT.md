# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Cocode to be a welcoming, respectful, and useful project for everyone.
Participants are expected to act professionally, assume good intent, and leave
room for different backgrounds, experiences, and technical opinions.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus on the problem, not on the person.
- Welcome questions, corrections, and differing viewpoints.
- Keep discussions relevant and avoid sharing private information.
- Follow the project's security reporting process for vulnerabilities.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, sexualized language or
attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing someone else's private
information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, pull requests,
and other contributions that violate this code. They may also temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, contact the project
maintainers through the repository's private contact channel. Do not use the
public issue tracker for sensitive reports. Security vulnerabilities must follow
the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
